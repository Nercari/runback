### Added

- **Coach or stay quiet after a fight (J1).** A new bridge gate looks at a finished fight before a review: deaths, wipes, bosses and long fights are coached by code, trivial fights are quiet, and only middle cases ask Jev one question of plain numbers (no names or text). Setting `forever.coachGate.mode`: `off`, `shadow` (default, logs only) or `on`. Any Jev failure runs the mentor as before.
